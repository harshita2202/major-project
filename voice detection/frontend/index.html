Microphone & voice processing consent

To keep this exam fair, VoiceGuard verifies that only you are speaking while you take the test.

  • What we capture: your microphone audio during enrollment (~20 s) and throughout the exam.
  • How it's used: audio is analysed in real time to compute a numeric voice profile. Raw audio is not stored.
  • Exception: when another voice is detected, a short clip (a few seconds) is saved as evidence for the proctor.
  • Retention: your voice profile and session data are deleted from memory as soon as the exam ends.
